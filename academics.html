Academic Excellence

Comprehensive curriculum designed to prepare students for success in college and beyond

Our Academic Philosophy

At D-Broft High School, we believe that every student has the potential to excel academically. Our comprehensive curriculum is designed to challenge students while providing the support they need to succeed. We offer a balanced approach that combines rigorous academics with practical skills, critical thinking, and character development.

Our academic programs are structured to meet diverse learning styles and interests, ensuring that every student finds their path to success. From Advanced Placement courses to career-focused programs, we provide opportunities for all students to reach their full potential.

Academic Departments

English Language Arts

Our English department focuses on developing strong communication skills, critical thinking, and literary analysis. Students explore classic and contemporary literature while honing their writing and speaking abilities.

  • AP English Literature & Composition
  • AP English Language & Composition
  • Creative Writing
  • Journalism & Media Studies
  • Public Speaking & Debate

Mathematics

Department Head: Dr. Robert Kim

Our mathematics program builds strong foundational skills and prepares students for advanced study in STEM fields. We emphasize problem-solving, logical reasoning, and real-world applications.

  • AP Calculus AB & BC
  • AP Statistics
  • Algebra I & II
  • Geometry & Trigonometry
  • Pre-Calculus

Science

  • AP Biology, Chemistry, Physics
  • Environmental Science
  • Anatomy & Physiology
  • Earth & Space Science
  • Forensic Science

Social Studies

  • AP U.S. History & Government

World Languages

Special Academic Programs

STEM Academy

Our STEM Academy provides intensive study in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. Students engage in project-based learning, research opportunities, and partnerships with local universities and tech companies.

Features:

  • Robotics and Engineering Design
  • Computer Science and Programming
  • Research Projects and Science Fair
  • Industry Partnerships and Internships

Advanced Placement (AP)

We offer 18 AP courses across all academic disciplines, allowing students to earn college credit while still in high school. Our AP pass rate consistently exceeds national averages.

Available AP Courses:

  • 18 AP courses offered
  • 85% average pass rate
  • Dedicated AP preparation programs
  • College credit opportunities

Career & Technical Education

Our CTE programs prepare students for immediate entry into the workforce or continued education in technical fields. Students gain hands-on experience and industry certifications.

Programs Include:

  • Business & Entrepreneurship
  • Health Sciences
  • Information Technology
  • Automotive Technology

Dual Enrollment

Through partnerships with local colleges, students can earn both high school and college credit simultaneously. This program allows motivated students to get a head start on their college education.

Benefits:

  • College credit while in high school
  • Reduced college costs
  • College-level academic experience
  • Accelerated degree completion

Academic Support Services

We believe every student can succeed with the right support. Our comprehensive academic support services ensure that all students have the resources they need to excel.

Tutoring Center

Free peer and teacher tutoring available in all subjects. Students can receive individual or small group support to master challenging concepts.

Academic Counseling

Our counselors work with students to develop four-year academic plans, select appropriate courses, and prepare for college and career success.

Technology Support

1:1 device program ensures every student has access to technology. Technical support and digital literacy training are provided.

Study Skills

Workshops and courses on effective study strategies, time management, note-taking, and test preparation help students develop essential academic skills.
