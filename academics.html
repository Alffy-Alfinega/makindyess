Our Academic Philosophy

At Makindye Secondary School, we are committed to providing quality education that prepares students for the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) and Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) examinations. Our curriculum follows the national syllabus while incorporating innovative teaching methods to ensure academic excellence and holistic development.

We offer both O-Level (S1-S4) and A-Level (S5-S6) programs, with students able to choose from various combinations including Sciences, Arts, and Technical subjects. Our dedicated faculty members employ student-centered approaches to foster critical thinking, creativity, and practical skills.

Special Academic Programs

Science Club

Our Science Club engages students in practical experiments, science fairs, and competitions. Members participate in national science congresses and develop research skills.

Activities:

  • Practical experiments and demonstrations
  • Science fairs and exhibitions
  • National science competitions
  • Field trips to research institutions

Tutorial Program

Our tutorial program provides additional academic support for students who need extra help. Qualified teachers offer after-school tutoring in all major subjects.

Services:

  • Subject-specific tutoring
  • Exam preparation support
  • Study skills development
  • Individualized learning plans

ICT Program

Our Information and Communication Technology program equips students with digital skills essential for the modern world. Students learn computer applications and programming.

Focus Areas:

  • Computer literacy
  • Word processing and spreadsheets
  • Internet research skills
  • Basic programming concepts

Leadership Development

Our leadership program develops student leaders through prefect systems, clubs, and community service. Students learn responsibility, organization, and public speaking.

Components:

  • Prefect system and student government
  • Club leadership roles
  • Community service projects
  • Public speaking and debate

Academic Support Services

We are committed to ensuring every student succeeds academically. Our support services provide the necessary assistance for students to achieve their full potential.

Remedial Classes

Additional teaching sessions are provided for students who need extra help in specific subjects. Qualified teachers offer targeted support to improve understanding.

Academic Guidance

Our guidance counselors help students choose appropriate subject combinations, develop study plans, and prepare for national examinations.

Computer Access

Students have access to computer facilities for research, assignments, and ICT education. Internet access is available for academic purposes.

Library Services

Our library provides textbooks, reference materials, and study spaces. Librarians assist students with research and reading programs.
