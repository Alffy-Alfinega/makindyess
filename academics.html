Our Academic Programs

Rigorous Education for the 21st Century

Academic Excellence at Makindye

Makindye Secondary School offers a comprehensive curriculum based on the Ugandan National Curriculum for O-Level and A-Level education. We follow the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) standards while maintaining an internationally welcoming environment. Our programs integrate traditional teaching methods with modern pedagogical approaches, including ICT-enhanced learning, experiential education, and collaborative projects. Students benefit from small class sizes, personalized attention, and rigorous academic standards that prepare them for both local and international opportunities.

Curriculum & International Students

Ugandan Curriculum:
O-Level (Senior 1-4) and A-Level (Senior 5-6) following UNEB standards and guidelines

International Welcome:
We warmly welcome international students and provide support for adaptation to Ugandan educational standards

Academic Levels & Streams

Following the Ugandan National Curriculum, Makindye offers two main academic levels:

O-Level
(Senior 1-4)

Four-year foundational program covering core subjects and electives. Students develop broad knowledge across Science, Arts, and Commercial disciplines before specialization.

A-Level
(Senior 5-6)
