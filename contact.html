Visit Our Campus

Schedule a Tour

We welcome prospective students and families to visit our campus and see what makes Makindye Secondary School special.

Individual Tours: Available by appointment Monday-Friday

Group Tours: Every Saturday at 10:00 AM

Shadow Days: Prospective students can spend a day with current students

To schedule a visit, call our Admissions Office at +256 414 123 460 or email admissions@makindyess.sc.ug

Directions & Parking

From Downtown: Take Main Street north for 2 miles, turn right on Education Street

From Highway 101: Exit at Learning City Boulevard, follow signs to Education District

Public Transportation: Bus routes 15 and 22 stop directly in front of the school

Visitor Parking: Available in the main parking lot. Please check in at the main office upon arrival.
