About D-Broft High School

Discover our story, mission, and commitment to educational excellence

Our Story

Founded in 1974, D-Broft High School has been a cornerstone of educational excellence in our community for over 50 years. What began as a small institution with just 200 students has grown into a thriving academic community of over 1,200 students and 85 dedicated faculty members.

Our journey has been marked by continuous innovation, unwavering commitment to student success, and a deep understanding that education extends far beyond textbooks and classrooms. We believe in nurturing not just academic achievement, but also character development, critical thinking, and the values that will guide our students throughout their lives.

Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive, innovative, and inclusive educational experience that empowers every student to achieve academic excellence, develop strong character, and become responsible global citizens prepared for success in college, career, and life.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a leading educational institution that inspires lifelong learning, fosters creativity and innovation, and cultivates leaders who will make positive contributions to their communities and the world.

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Respect, Innovation, Collaboration, and Community. These core values guide every decision we make and every interaction we have with our students, families, and community.

Our Achievements

Academic Excellence

Consistently ranked among the top 10% of high schools in the state for academic performance and student achievement.

National Recognition

Recipient of the National Blue Ribbon School Award for outstanding academic achievement and progress in closing achievement gaps.

STEM Excellence

Our STEM programs have won multiple state and national competitions, with students regularly advancing to international science fairs.

College Readiness

95% of our graduates are accepted to four-year colleges, with many receiving merit-based scholarships and attending prestigious universities.

Our Facilities

D-Broft High School boasts state-of-the-art facilities designed to support 21st-century learning and student development:

Science Laboratories

Modern chemistry, physics, and biology labs equipped with the latest technology and safety features.

Technology Center

Computer labs with high-speed internet, 3D printers, and robotics equipment for hands-on STEM learning.

Library & Media Center

A comprehensive library with over 15,000 books, digital resources, and collaborative study spaces.

Athletic Facilities

Full gymnasium, outdoor track, soccer field, and tennis courts supporting our comprehensive athletics program.

Performing Arts Center

A 500-seat auditorium with professional lighting and sound systems for theater, music, and dance performances.

Cafeteria & Commons

Spacious dining area serving nutritious meals with options for various dietary needs and preferences.

Community Involvement

At D-Broft High School, we believe education extends beyond our campus walls. Our students, faculty, and families are actively engaged in community service and local partnerships:

  • Annual community service requirement of 40 hours for all students
  • Partnerships with local businesses for internship and mentorship opportunities
  • Environmental sustainability initiatives including recycling and energy conservation programs
  • Support for local food banks and charitable organizations
  • Collaboration with elementary and middle schools for tutoring and mentoring programs
  • Regular community forums and town halls to discuss educational priorities
