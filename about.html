About Makindye

Shaping Leaders Since 1960

Our Story

Makindye Secondary School was established in 1994 as a vision to provide quality secondary education in Uganda. Founded on the principles of academic excellence, moral development, and community responsibility, the school has evolved into one of Uganda's premier educational institutions. Located in Makindye, Kampala, the school sits on a spacious, serene campus that provides an ideal learning environment.

For over six decades, Makindye has been committed to nurturing young minds and developing well-rounded individuals who contribute positively to society. Our tradition of excellence has produced countless alumni who have gone on to achieve great success in various fields both locally and internationally.

Our Pillars

Mission

To strive for Development through academic excellence, Quality Discipline and Smartness.

Vision

To be the leading and most reliable secondary school in shaping and grooming of an academically sound morally upright and socially participate future citizens.

Core Values

Integrity, Excellence, Inclusivity, Compassion, Accountability, Innovation, and Community Service - these guide all we do.

What We Stand For

Academic Excellence

We believe in rigorous intellectual development. Our curriculum challenges students to think critically, problem-solve creatively, and achieve their academic potential at both local and international standards.

Character & Leadership

Beyond textbooks, we cultivate ethical leaders. Through mentorship, community service, and values-based programs, we develop young people with integrity and the courage to make a positive difference.

Holistic Development

We nurture the whole person - intellectually, physically, artistically, emotionally, and spiritually through sports, music, drama, debate, and service programs.

Global Citizenship

In an interconnected world, we prepare students to view themselves as global citizens who care about their communities and contribute to solving global challenges.
