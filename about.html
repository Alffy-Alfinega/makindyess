About Makindye Secondary School

Discover our story, mission, and commitment to educational excellence

Our Story

Makindye Secondary School, established in 1965, has been a beacon of educational excellence in Kampala, Uganda for over five decades. Located in the heart of Makindye Division, our school has grown from a modest institution to a leading secondary school serving over 1,500 students from diverse backgrounds.

Throughout our history, we have maintained a strong commitment to academic excellence, moral development, and community service. Our alumni have gone on to become leaders in various fields including medicine, engineering, law, education, and business, making significant contributions to Uganda's development.

As a government-aided mixed secondary school, we pride ourselves on providing quality education that combines traditional Ugandan values with modern teaching methodologies, preparing students for both national examinations and global opportunities.

Our Mission

To provide quality secondary education that develops well-rounded individuals equipped with knowledge, skills, and values necessary for academic success, personal growth, and contribution to national development.

Our Vision

To be a model secondary school in Uganda that produces academically excellent, morally upright, and socially responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Discipline, Respect, Patriotism, and Community Service. These core values guide our daily operations and shape the character of our students.

Our Achievements

Academic Excellence

Consistently high performance in Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) and Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) examinations, with many students qualifying for university admission.

National Recognition

Recognized by the Uganda Ministry of Education for outstanding performance in sciences and humanities, contributing to national development goals.

STEM Excellence

Our science students have excelled in national science competitions and many have pursued careers in medicine, engineering, and technology.

University Success

Over 85% of our graduates gain admission to universities across East Africa and beyond, with many receiving government scholarships.

Our Facilities

Makindye Secondary School provides modern facilities designed to support comprehensive education and student development:

Science Laboratories

Well-equipped chemistry, physics, and biology laboratories with modern apparatus and safety equipment for practical science education.

Computer Laboratory

Computer lab with internet access and educational software supporting ICT education and digital literacy programs.

Library

A comprehensive library with textbooks, reference materials, and study spaces supporting research and independent learning.

Sports Facilities

Football field, basketball court, and other sports facilities promoting physical education and healthy competition.

Music & Arts Room

Dedicated spaces for music, art, and cultural activities fostering creativity and cultural appreciation.

Dining Hall

School cafeteria providing nutritious meals and promoting healthy eating habits among students.

Community Involvement

At Makindye Secondary School, we believe education extends beyond our campus walls. Our students, faculty, and families are actively engaged in community service and local partnerships:

  • Annual community service requirement of 40 hours for all students
  • Partnerships with local businesses for internship and mentorship opportunities
  • Environmental sustainability initiatives including recycling and energy conservation programs
  • Support for local food banks and charitable organizations
  • Collaboration with elementary and middle schools for tutoring and mentoring programs
  • Regular community forums and town halls to discuss educational priorities
