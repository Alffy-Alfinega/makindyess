News & Events

Stay updated with the latest happenings at Makindye Secondary School

Latest News

15 Feb

New Academic Year Begins

The 2025 academic year has officially commenced! We welcome back our continuing students and extend a warm welcome to our new Senior 1 and Senior 5 students. The school is fully prepared with updated facilities and enthusiastic staff ready to guide our students towards academic excellence.

Posted by: Mr. Joseph Mukasa, Headteacher

10 Feb

UCE Results Celebration

Congratulations to our Senior 4 students who excelled in the 2024 Uganda Certificate of Education examinations! Over 95% of our candidates qualified for A-Level studies, with several students achieving Division 1 results. Special recognition goes to our top performers in Sciences and Humanities.

Posted by: Ms. Grace Nakato, Deputy Headteacher

05 Feb

Inter-House Sports Competition

Our annual inter-house sports competitions have begun with great enthusiasm! Teams from all houses are competing in football, basketball, and athletics. The competitions promote teamwork, sportsmanship, and healthy competition among our students.

Posted by: Mr. David Okello, Sports Coordinator

10 Sep

Science Fair Winners Announced

Congratulations to our talented students who excelled in this year's annual science fair! First place went to Emma Chen for her project on renewable energy solutions. Second place was awarded to Marcus Johnson for his research on water purification methods.

Posted by: Ms. Jennifer Williams, Science Fair Coordinator

08 Sep

New Art Gallery Exhibition

The Makindye Secondary School Art Gallery is proud to present "Student Visions," featuring artwork from our talented visual arts students. The exhibition showcases paintings, sculptures, and digital media pieces created during the school art program.

Posted by: Ms. Lisa Chang, Art Department Head

05 Sep

Parent-Teacher Conference Scheduled

Our first parent-teacher conference of the year is scheduled for September 20-21. This is an excellent opportunity for parents to meet with teachers, discuss student progress, and learn about upcoming academic goals. Please sign up for your preferred time slot.

Posted by: Ms. Patricia Johnson, Student Services
