Our Resource Center

Makindye Secondary School provides comprehensive learning resources to support academic success and personal development. Our resource center includes physical library materials, digital databases, tutoring support, and counseling services readily available to all students.

Library & Archives

Our comprehensive library houses over 15,000 books, journals, and reference materials covering all subject areas. Students have access to digital databases and e-books.
