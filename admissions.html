Admissions

Join Our Community of Excellence

Welcome to Makindye

Makindye Secondary School welcomes applications from qualified students seeking a dynamic, academically rigorous, and supportive learning environment. We serve students from all regions of Uganda and accept a diverse student body committed to academic excellence and personal growth.

Admission Process

Step 1: Obtain Application Form

Request an application form from the school office or access it online. The form requires basic personal information and academic history.

Step 2: Complete Application

Fill out the form completely with accurate information. Attach required documents including PLE certificates, birth certificates, and character references.

Step 3: Submit Application

Submit your completed form with all supporting documents to the admissions office. The deadline for applications is [Date].

Step 4: Entrance Exam

Shortlisted candidates sit for entry examinations in English, Mathematics, and one elective subject as per stream choice.

Step 5: Interview

Successful exam candidates are invited for a personal interview with the admissions panel to assess character and motivation.

Step 6: Selection & Confirmation

Successful candidates receive admission letters and are required to confirm acceptance and payment of fees within two weeks.

Requirements & Fees

Entry Requirements

  • Senior 1 Admission:
    Uganda PLE Pass with aggregates 1-2 or equivalent qualification
  • Senior 3 Admission:
    UCE or O-Level Pass (Grade C and above) in relevant subjects
  • Senior 5 Admission:
    UACE or A-Level Pass with minimum D+ grades
  • Other Requirements:
    Birth certificate, character references, medical report

School Fees 2026

  • Day Student (per term):
    UGX 600,000
  • Boarder (per term):
    UGX 1,400,000
  • Admission Fee (one-time):
    UGX 50,000
  • Note:
    Payment plans and scholarships available for qualified students

Scholarships & Support

Makindye is committed to ensuring deserving students access quality education. We offer various support programs:

Merit Scholarships

Full or partial tuition coverage for students demonstrating exceptional academic performance or talent in sports/arts.

Need-Based Support

Financial assistance for qualified students from disadvantaged backgrounds who meet academic standards.

Bursaries Program

Partial fee support through partnerships with NGOs, corporations, and philanthropic organizations.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the admission deadline?

Applications for the 2026 academic year close on [Date]. We recommend submitting early as some streams may close when capacity is reached.

Can I choose my academic stream after admission?

Yes, subject to your entry qualifications and performance in placement tests. However, we recommend deciding based on your strengths and career aspirations.

Is boarding compulsory?

No. We offer both day and boarding options. Students can choose based on preference and distance from school.

What is the school environment like for students with special needs?

Makindye is committed to inclusion. We provide support for students with documented learning differences and physical disabilities. Contact admissions to discuss specific needs.
