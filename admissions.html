Join the Makindye Secondary School community and begin your journey to academic excellence

Welcome to Makindye Secondary School Admissions

We are excited that you are considering Makindye Secondary School for your secondary education. Our admissions process is designed to welcome students who demonstrate academic potential and a commitment to personal development.

As a government-aided mixed secondary school, we admit students based on their Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) results and other relevant criteria. We strive to provide quality education that prepares students for both further education and life skills.

Application Process

Step 1: PLE Results

Obtain your Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) results from the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB). Results are usually available in January.

Minimum requirement: Division 1-3 for admission

Step 2: School Application

Submit application form to the school with PLE results, birth certificate, medical records, and parent/guardian information.

Application period: January - February

Step 3: Selection Interview

Shortlisted candidates attend an interview with school administrators to assess suitability and discuss school expectations.

Interview period: February - March

Step 4: Admission Offer

Successful candidates receive admission letters with enrollment instructions, fee payment details, and orientation information.

Notification: By end of March

Admission Requirements

Academic Requirements

  • Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) Certificate
  • Minimum Division 1-3 aggregate
  • Completion of Primary 7
  • Age 12-15 years for S1 admission
  • Good conduct certificate from previous school

Required Documents

  • PLE Results Slip
  • Birth Certificate
  • Medical Report/Immunization Card
  • Transfer Letter from previous school
  • Parent/Guardian Identification
  • Recent Passport-size Photos (2)

Important Dates

  • January: PLE Results Released
  • January-February: Applications Open
  • February-March: Selection Interviews
  • March: Admission Offers
  • Early February: New Academic Year Begins

School Fees

  • Tuition fees vary by category
  • Day students: UGX 200,000-400,000 per term
  • Boarding students: UGX 600,000-800,000 per term
  • Functional fees: UGX 50,000-100,000 per term
  • Scholarships available for needy students

Scholarships & Financial Support

Makindye Secondary School is committed to ensuring that financial constraints do not prevent deserving students from accessing quality education. We offer various forms of financial support.

Academic Scholarships

Excellence Scholarship

Full or partial fee waivers for students with outstanding PLE results (Division 1)

Subject Prizes

Awards for top performers in specific subjects at UCE/UACE

Need-Based Support

Universal Secondary Education (USE)

Government program providing free education for four years of secondary schooling

School Fee Subsidy

Reduced fees for students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds

Special Categories

Girls' Education Support

Additional support for female students to promote gender equality in education

Disability Support

Special assistance and fee concessions for students with disabilities

Community Programs

Community Service Awards

Recognition and support for students actively involved in community development

Leadership Development

Support for student leaders in prefect systems and clubs
